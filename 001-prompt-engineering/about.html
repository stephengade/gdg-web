THE ORIGIN STORY

Born on 24th Street in San Francisco's Mission District.

In the winter of 2024, Mateo Cruz—a former fine-dining sous chef—watched a 45-day dry-aged wagyu smash burger sit on a stainless steel pass for 37 minutes.

The third-party app courier was stuck three neighborhoods away picking up orders from two chain convenience stores and a bubble tea shop. When the driver finally arrived, he shoved Mateo's creation into a lukewarm nylon backpack already bulging with cold sodas.

That night, Mateo met with Liam Chen, an urban logistics software architect. On the back of a stained butcher paper receipt, they sketched the Cravely thesis: "1 Kitchen. 1 Dedicated Courier. 1 Active Heated Induction Pod. Under 2.2 Miles. 20 Minutes Flat."

"If an artist spent three days cultivating sourdough or forty-five days aging beef, an algorithm has no right to deliver it forty minutes late and cold."
— Mateo Cruz & Liam Chen, Co-Founders of Cravely

CULINARY INTEGRITY

The Four Non-Negotiable Pillars

Every operational rule at Cravely was designed to protect the flavor profile of the dish and the livelihood of the independent chef.

01

The 2.2-Mile Micro-Hub Radius

We never accept orders beyond a 2.2-mile radius from an indie kitchen. Hot food has an unbendable biological half-life. By limiting delivery distance, our electric cargo couriers reach any doorstep in under 12 minutes of ride time.

02

Ceramic Induction Thermal Pods

Standard insulated bags trap moisture, turning crispy buns and pizza crusts into soggy rubber. Cravely’s patented induction pods actively heat at 155°F while venting humidity, maintaining the exact crispness and temperature of a dining room plate.

03

Chef-First Fair Economics

The legacy aggregators take 30% to 40% from restaurants while hitting diners with surprise service fees. Cravely caps commissions at a transparent 15%, ensuring independent culinary masters earn the lion’s share of every meal they craft.

04

Direct-Flight Courier Guild

Zero multi-order stacking. Zero intermediate stops. When courier Marco picks up your Margherita pizza from North Beach, he is routed directly to your door without detouring for someone else's groceries.
